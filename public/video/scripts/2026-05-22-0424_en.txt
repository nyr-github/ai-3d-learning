Recently I noticed

lots of projects turning images into 3D models with AI

They look amazing

But most models end up sitting idle on hard drives

So I decided

to use a complete Chinese AI toolchain

to build a full online showcase solution

I used Doubao to generate beautiful cell images

Then Hunyuan 3D to convert them into GLB models

And with Qoder

I built this interactive 3D showcase website step by step

Now you can open a webpage

rotate a plant cell 360 degrees

zoom in to see mitochondria structures

explore the DNA double helix

Every model is fully interactive

Not just static display

But also with detailed teaching notes

exam focus points

microscope visibility guides

All the code is completely open source

With simple configuration

you can adapt it to your own projects

Showcase your products

your designs

your creativity

Finally

I hope this tool helps you

turn AI-generated 3D models

into shareable online experiences

and let your creations truly be seen
