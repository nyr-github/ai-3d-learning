Students struggle to understand 3D structures from 2D textbooks. Teachers need better visualization tools for classroom demonstrations. Commercial 3D software costs hundreds of dollars per license.

Show3D solves these problems with a free, browser-based platform that brings scientific models to life in interactive 3D. Rotate, zoom, and explore cells, molecules, and structures instantly. Rich educational metadata including test points, fun facts, and microscope visibility info supports real learning.

Key features: Interactive 3D viewer with full controls, Hollywood-style presentation mode, built-in format converter (OBJ/FBX/STL to GLB), professional model optimizer (50-80% size reduction), intelligent caching for instant loading, responsive design for all devices.

Currently includes biology content (cells, organelles, molecules, viruses) with extensible architecture for chemistry, physics, and more. Built with Next.js and Three.js, deploys to any CDN. MIT licensed, completely free forever.

Perfect for teachers, students, and self-learners. Zero installation, zero registration, zero cost. Science education, reimagined for the 3D era.
