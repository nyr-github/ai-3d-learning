Education technology should democratize access, not create barriers. This platform delivers professional-grade 3D visualization completely free and without registration. Built on Next.js with static site generation, it deploys to any CDN for lightning-fast global access. The extensible architecture supports unlimited disciplines - biology, chemistry, physics, engineering - all through a unified data model. Teachers get powerful tools: highlighted test points, size range sliders, and attribute panels. Students get an intuitive exploration experience with auto-rotation, fullscreen mode, and instant model switching. The value proposition is clear: premium 3D education tools, open-source and accessible to all.
