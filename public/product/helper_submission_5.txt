Imagine learning about cell biology by actually rotating a 3D plant cell, zooming into chloroplasts, and examining the thylakoid structure up close. That's the Show3D experience - learning through hands-on exploration, not passive reading.

The platform makes 3D scientific models instantly accessible: browse projects on the homepage, click a model, and watch it load in seconds. Then explore freely - rotate with mouse or touch, zoom in to see details, pan to examine from any angle. Toggle auto-rotation for passive viewing, or activate Hollywood red carpet mode for a slow, cinematic 360° presentation. Switch between models instantly to compare structures (plant cell vs. animal cell, different virus types) - the intelligent caching makes switching feel immediate.

The info panel transforms exploration into education. Read detailed scientific descriptions. Discover fun facts that spark curiosity ("Mitochondria have their own DNA!"). Review teaching focus points aligned with curricula. Examine key structures marked as high-frequency test points. Check microscope visibility for lab prep. Explore size ranges with interactive sliders showing realistic scale.

Mobile experience is equally polished: full-screen 3D viewer, floating control buttons, slide-out drawers for sidebar and info, touch-optimized gestures that feel natural. Study on your phone during commutes, in bed before sleep, or anywhere learning strikes.

Built-in tools extend the value: convert your own 3D files (OBJ, FBX, STL, etc.) to optimized GLB format, or compress existing GLB files by 50-80% while maintaining visual quality. Analyze model capabilities including polygon counts, textures, animations, and bones.

No signup, no download, no payment. Just open a browser and start exploring. Science education should be engaging, interactive, and accessible to everyone. Show3D delivers exactly that.
