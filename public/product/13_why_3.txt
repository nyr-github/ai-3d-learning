Education inequality is real. Schools in wealthy areas afford expensive 3D software and lab equipment while underfunded schools use outdated textbooks. This creates a two-tier system where resources, not ability, determine learning outcomes.

We built this platform to level that playing field. By combining modern web technology with open-source values, we created professional-quality 3D educational tools accessible to anyone with a browser - no expensive software, no high-end hardware, no institutional subscriptions.

Our motivation is simple: every student deserves access to quality educational resources, regardless of their zip code or family income. Technology should democratize education, not create new barriers. This platform proves that free, open-source, and high-quality can coexist.
