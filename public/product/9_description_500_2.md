# Learn Biology Through Interactive 3D Exploration

## Why We Built This
As a biology student, I constantly struggled to visualize complex cellular structures from flat textbook images. How do chloroplasts arrange inside a plant cell? What does a bacteriophage actually look like? These questions drove me to build a solution.

## What You Can Do
This platform puts interactive 3D models directly in your browser:
- **Explore** plant and animal cells from every angle
- **Rotate** DNA double helices to understand base pairing
- **Examine** virus structures in detailed 3D
- **Zoom** into mitochondria and chloroplasts

Every model includes rich educational context:
- Teaching focus points highlight what's important for exams
- Fun facts spark curiosity and deeper learning
- Microscope visibility guides prepare you for lab work

## Designed for Real Learning
Teachers can project it in classrooms, students can study on their phones during commutes, and self-learners can explore at their own pace. No downloads, no accounts, no paywalls - just pure, accessible science education.
