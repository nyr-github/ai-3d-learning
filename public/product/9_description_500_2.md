## Make Science Learning Engaging

Learning science should be **engaging, not frustrating**. Students deserve to rotate a DNA helix, zoom into a cell membrane, or explore molecular structures with their fingers.

### User Experience

That's why we created this **free, open-source platform**:

1. **Select** a model from the sidebar
2. **Watch** it load instantly with smart caching
3. **Interact** with every angle using touch or mouse
4. **Learn** with rich educational metadata

Each model includes fun facts, test points, and microscope visibility info. The responsive design works perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops.
