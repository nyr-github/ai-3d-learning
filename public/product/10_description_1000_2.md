## Transform How Students Experience Science

Learning science should be **engaging, not frustrating**. For decades, students have struggled to understand complex 3D structures from flat textbooks. How can you truly grasp the double helix structure of DNA, the fluid mosaic model of cell membranes, or the intricate anatomy of a bacteriophage from 2D diagrams?

### The Student Experience

That's why we created this **free, open-source platform** - to put powerful 3D exploration tools in every student's hands:

**Instant Exploration:**
1. Browse projects on the homepage (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and more)
2. Click any project card to enter the interactive viewer
3. Select models from the organized sidebar with category filters
4. Watch models load instantly with our smart caching system
5. Rotate, zoom, and pan with intuitive mouse or touch controls

**Rich Educational Context:**
Each model comes with comprehensive metadata designed by educators:
- **Detailed descriptions** explaining biological/chemical significance
- **Fun facts** that spark curiosity and real-world connections
- **Teaching focus** highlighting key learning objectives
- **Key structures** with high-frequency test point markers
- **Microscope visibility** information for lab preparation
- **Size range sliders** showing realistic scale with proper units
- **Attribute panels** with domain-specific data (cell type, energy type, etc.)

**Designed for Every Device:**
The responsive interface adapts seamlessly:
- **Desktop**: Three-column resizable layout (sidebar 20%, viewer 55%, info panel 25%)
- **Mobile**: Full-screen 3D viewer with slide-out drawers and floating control buttons
- **Tablet**: Optimized touch interactions with gesture-based navigation

**Hollywood-Style Presentation:**
Experience our signature "red carpet" mode - models perform a slow, elegant rotation showcasing every angle, perfect for classroom demonstrations or presentations.

**Built-in Tools:**
- Convert 3D files (OBJ, FBX, STL, etc.) to optimized GLB format
- Compress existing GLB models with one click
- Analyze model capabilities (polygons, textures, animations, bones)

No registration required. No paywalls. Just pure, interactive science education.
