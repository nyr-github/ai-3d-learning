Learning science should be engaging, not frustrating. Students deserve to rotate a DNA helix, zoom into a cell membrane, or explore molecular structures with their fingers. That's why we created this free, open-source platform. Simply select a model from the sidebar, and watch it load instantly with our smart caching system. Interact with every angle using touch or mouse controls. Each model comes with rich educational metadata including fun facts, test points, and microscope visibility info. The responsive design works perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops - making science accessible everywhere.
