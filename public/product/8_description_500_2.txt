As a biology student, I constantly struggled to visualize complex cellular structures from flat textbook images. How do chloroplasts arrange inside a plant cell? What does a bacteriophage actually look like? These questions drove me to build a solution.

This platform puts interactive 3D models directly in your browser. Explore plant and animal cells, rotate DNA double helices, examine virus structures, and zoom into mitochondria - all with smooth, intuitive controls. Every model includes rich educational context: teaching focus points highlight what's important for exams, fun facts spark curiosity, and microscope visibility guides prepare you for lab work.

The platform is designed for real learning scenarios. Teachers can project it in classrooms, students can study on their phones during commutes, and self-learners can explore at their own pace. No downloads, no accounts, no paywalls - just pure, accessible science education powered by open-source technology and a passion for making learning visual and engaging.
